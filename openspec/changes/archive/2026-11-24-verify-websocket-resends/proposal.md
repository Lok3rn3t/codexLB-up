## Why

A reconnect can select a cached completion from a different physical WebSocket even when the client has supplied a complete portable turn. An unmatched or duplicated tool result can also make an anchorless retry invalid.

## What Changes

Keep a verified portable resend intact on a different connection. Before replaying without a parent, check that historical tool results match the calls that produced them. Retain caller-supplied anchors and ownership checks.

## Impact

Direct Codex Responses WebSockets and the shared tool-pair check used for recovery.
